## Description

MyAFBase is the unofficial installation companion for U.S. Air Force Airmen. Gates, resources, readiness tools, and career planning in one native app.

Whether you're in-processing, stationed, or preparing to PCS, MyAFBase helps you navigate your base and stay ahead of the deadlines that matter — without spreadsheets, group chats, or another login.

YOUR BASE, AT A GLANCE
• 85 Air Force installations — gates, dining, fitness, medical, events, and emergency contacts
• See what's open now with live hours and status
• Explore on Apple Maps with base-scoped search
• Pull to refresh for updated hours, gate status, and alerts — no app update required
• Bookmark gates and resources to your Home dashboard

STAY READY
• Personal readiness tracker for PFRA, dental, CAC, immunizations, and custom due dates
• Reminders tab surfaces upcoming deadlines and award package suspenses
• PCS checklists and assignment phases (In Processing, Stationed, Out Processing)
• Home Screen widgets: readiness countdown, weather, open-now picks, pay dates, emergency contacts, and more

BUILT-IN TOOLS
• PFRA Score Calculator, Goal Planner, and Records — estimate scores, plan targets, and track trends
• Leave Planner — multi-trip planning, balance projection, and PCS leave caps
• Pay Calendar — mid-month, month-end, and special pays with weekend pay-gap warnings
• Essential AFI Search — dress, leave, fitness, and more offline with citations to official PDFs

WAR TRACKER
Stop scrambling at EPB/OPB closeout. Log accomplishments as they happen:
• Quick weekly log with categories, tags, and performance factors
• Reports for any date range — plain text, bullets, or PDF export
• Award deadline tracking with reminders
• Optional Face ID lock and PII warnings before export
• Home Screen widget and Siri shortcut for one-tap logging
• Stored only on your device — not connected to MyVector, vMPF, or official systems

DESIGNED FOR YOU
• Native iOS design across Home, Explore, Assignment, Reminders, and Menu
• Optional iCloud sync for bookmarks, assignment dates, readiness tracker, and checklists
• Phase-aware onboarding tailored to your assignment
• Works offline with bundled base data; syncs when you're back online
• Free — no subscription, no account required

IMPORTANT
MyAFBase is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or an official product of the U.S. Department of Defense, Department of the Air Force, or any military installation. All information is for planning and reference only — verify critical details through official channels.
