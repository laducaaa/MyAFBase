>
> **Item safeguard:** These are independently written study questions, not actual, recalled, reconstructed, or predictive WAPS questions.
>
> **Rank / ADTC use:** Sections 23A and 23B are B—Understanding for both SSgt and TSgt.
>
> **Volume posture:** Items test major rule families and distinctive distinctions—not every Table 23.1 row or badge-placement millimeter. Controlling AFH/DAFI dress guidance governs detail and must be revalidated for duty.
>
> **Source-study rule:** Preserve the 15 February 2025 AFH 1 edition for exam-source study. Current DAFI 36-2903/DAFI 52-201 control real duties.
>
> **Format:** Four-option, one-best-answer practice. “Difficulty” describes MyPromotion instructional design, not operational WAPS difficulty.
>
> **Wrapper governance:** Product-level non-affiliation language and release gates are governed by `study-materials/README.md` and the release wrapper.

### C23-Q-001

- **ADTC:** 23A, B—Understanding
- **Ranks:** SSgt; TSgt
- **Difficulty:** Foundational

What five elements comprise dress and personal appearance standards?

A. Rank, AFSC, TIS, TIG, and PME.  
B. Height, weight, age, and fitness only.  
C. Neatness, cleanliness, safety, uniformity, and military image.  
D. Fashion, comfort, individuality, trend, and color.  

**Correct answer: C**

**Rationale:** AFH 1 states: Neatness, cleanliness, safety, uniformity, and military image.

**Why the other options are incorrect:**

- **A:** Does not match the AFH 1 rule as printed.

- **D:** Does not match the AFH 1 rule as printed.
