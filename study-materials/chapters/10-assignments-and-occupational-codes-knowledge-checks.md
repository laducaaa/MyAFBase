## Section 10A — Eligibility and Restrictions

### C10-Q-001

- **ADTC:** 10A, B—Understanding
- **Ranks:** SSgt; TSgt
- **Difficulty:** Foundational

Why does AFH 1 say the USAF classifies and assigns Airmen worldwide?

A. Only to maximize overseas tour lengths for every Airman.
B. To ensure a high state of readiness to accomplish the mission through a coherent classification and placement system.
C. Only to guarantee join-spouse assignments for all military couples.
D. Only to replace volunteer preferences with random selection.

**Correct answer: B**

**Rationale:** AFH 1 states the USAF classifies and assigns Airmen worldwide to ensure a high state of readiness, using a coherent system to identify requirements and specialties, set prerequisites, and place qualified Airmen.

**Why the other options are incorrect:**

- **A:** Tour-length maximization is not the stated purpose.
- **C:** Join spouse is considered when criteria are met; it is not guaranteed.
- **D:** Voluntary assignment is preferred to the maximum extent possible.

**Source:** AFH 1, ¶10.1
**Review flag:** Standard citation verification
