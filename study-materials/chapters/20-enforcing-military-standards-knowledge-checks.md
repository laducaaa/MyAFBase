## Section 20A — Air Force Inspection System

### Core B—Understanding items (SSgt and TSgt)

### C20-Q-001

- **ADTC:** 20A, B—Understanding
- **Ranks:** SSgt; TSgt
- **Difficulty:** Foundational

According to AFH 1, what does the Department of the Air Force Inspection System (AFIS) provide commanders?

A. A credible, independent assessment process with which to measure the capabilities of assigned forces.  
B. A substitute for all unit training and readiness reporting.  
C. A political endorsement process for public affairs releases.  
D. A replacement for commander authority over the mission.  

**Correct answer: A**

**Rationale:** AFH 1 states AFIS provides commanders a credible, independent assessment process to measure capabilities of assigned forces.

**Why the other options are incorrect:**

- **B:** B: AFIS assesses; it does not replace unit training systems.
- **C:** C: Public affairs is addressed elsewhere; not AFIS purpose.
- **D:** D: AFIS strengthens commanders; it does not replace their authority.

**Source:** AFH 1, ¶20.1  
**Review flag:** Standard citation verification

D. Military formal EO complaint filed by a third party.  

**Correct answer: A**

**Rationale:** DRB reviews administrative discharges within 15 years; appearance is a statutory right. AFBCMR is highest records correction and requires exhausting other remedies; appearance there is discretionary.

**Why the other options are incorrect:**

- **B:** B: DRB allows statutory personal appearance.
- **C:** C: Gatekeepers are inspection schedulers.
- **D:** D: Third parties cannot file military EO complaints for others.

**Source:** AFH 1, ¶¶20.29–20.30
