### C08-Q-001

- **ADTC:** 8A, B—Understanding
- **Ranks:** SSgt; TSgt
- **Difficulty:** Foundational

What is the performance feedback assessment according to AFH 1?

A. A formal two-way communication between a rater and ratee to discuss standards, responsibilities, expectations, and goals.  
B. An informal peer counseling session that replaces the performance evaluation.  
C. A one-way written reprimand filed in the selection record.  
D. A promotion board brief prepared only by the senior rater.  

**Correct answer: A**

**Rationale:** AFH 1 defines the performance feedback assessment as a formal two-way communication between rater and ratee covering standards, responsibilities, expectations, and goals.

**Why the other options are incorrect:**

- **B:** It does not replace the evaluation and is not peer counseling.
- **C:** It is two-way developmental feedback, not a reprimand filed in the selection record.
- **D:** It is conducted by the rater with the ratee, not a senior-rater-only promotion brief.

**Source:** AFH 1, ¶8.1  
**Review flag:** Standard citation verification
