### C04-Q-001

- **ADTC:** 4A, B—Understanding
- **Ranks:** SSgt; TSgt
- **Difficulty:** Foundational

How does AFH 1 distinguish the air and space domains for operational planning?

A. Air is governed by astrodynamics, while space is governed by aerodynamics.
B. Air and space are treated as a single domain governed only by electronics and networks.
C. Air is the region where aerodynamics generally govern operations, while space is the region where astrodynamics generally govern operations.
D. Air is limited to aircraft below cloud decks, while space begins at national airspace boundaries.

**Correct answer: C**

**Why the other options are incorrect:**

- **A:** It reverses the aerodynamics and astrodynamics associations.
- **B:** Air and space are separately described; cyberspace is the networked information-environment domain.
- **D:** The source uses aerodynamic and astrodynamic governance, not cloud decks or national airspace boundaries.

**Source:** AFH 1, ¶4.1
**Review flag:** Domain definitions; revalidate wording against controlling AFH edition
