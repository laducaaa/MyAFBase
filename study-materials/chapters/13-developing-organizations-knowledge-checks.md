### C13-Q-001

- **ADTC:** 13A, B—Understanding
- **Ranks:** SSgt; TSgt
- **Difficulty:** Foundational

How does AFH 1 define organizational management and the ability it provides leaders?

A. Organizing, planning, leading, and controlling resources to achieve established goals, enabling effective decisions and issue resolution for the organization and its employees.  
B. Restricting resources to senior leaders so only headquarters can set goals and resolve conflicts.  
C. Measuring employee satisfaction alone without linking resources to established organizational goals.  
D. Delegating all planning to subordinates while leaders retain only ceremonial oversight of resources.

**Correct answer: A**

**Rationale:** Organizational management is organizing, planning, leading, and controlling resources within an entity to achieve established goals, giving leaders the ability to decide and resolve issues for the organization and employees.

**Why the other options are incorrect:**

- **B:** The source does not limit management to headquarters-only resource control.
- **C:** Satisfaction measurement alone is not the definition; the cycle links resources to goals.
- **D:** Leaders organize, plan, lead, and control; the source does not reduce the role to ceremony.


**Source:** AFH 1, ¶13.1
