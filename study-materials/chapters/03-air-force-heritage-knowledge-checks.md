## Section 3A — The Air Force and the Mid-1900s

### C03-Q-001

- **ADTC:** 3A, A—Remembering
- **Ranks:** SSgt; TSgt
- **Difficulty:** Foundational

Which chronology does AFH 1 associate with establishment of an independent USAF?

A. Congress created the Department of the Air Force on 8 July 1948, and General Spaatz established the USAF on 18 September 1949.
B. President Truman signed the National Security Act on 26 July 1947, and the USAF was established on 18 September 1947.
C. The Women’s Armed Services Integration Act created the USAF on 12 June 1948, and Spaatz became Chief of Staff on 26 July 1947.
D. President Truman signed the National Security Act on 18 September 1947, and the USAF was established on 26 July 1948.

**Correct answer: B**

**Rationale:** AFH 1 states that on 26 July 1947 President Truman signed the National Security Act of 1947 creating a separate Department of the Air Force, and that on 18 September 1947 the USAF was officially established as an independent, equal branch.

**Why the other options are incorrect:**

- **A:** The Department of the Air Force is tied to the 1947 National Security Act, not to 8 July 1948, and the USAF establishment date is 18 September 1947.
- **C:** The Women’s Armed Services Integration Act concerned women’s permanent status; it did not create the USAF.
- **D:** It reverses the National Security Act and USAF establishment dates.

**Source:** AFH 1, ¶3.1
**Review flag:** Chronology verification
