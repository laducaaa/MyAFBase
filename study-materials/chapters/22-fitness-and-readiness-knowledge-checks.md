### C22-Q-001

- **ADTC:** 22A, B—Understanding
- **Ranks:** SSgt; TSgt
- **Difficulty:** Foundational

According to AFH 1, what is the goal of the Physical Fitness Program (PFP)?

A. Motivate all Airmen to participate in a year-round physical conditioning program emphasizing total fitness, including aerobic conditioning, muscular fitness training, and healthy eating.  
B. Replace all unit training with a single annual assessment day.  
C. Eliminate nutrition considerations from readiness.  
D. Restrict fitness participation to competitive athletes only.  

**Correct answer: A**
