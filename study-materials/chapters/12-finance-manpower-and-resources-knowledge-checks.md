## Section 12A — Individual Finances and Allowances

### C12-Q-001

- **ADTC:** 12A, B—Understanding
- **Ranks:** SSgt; TSgt
- **Difficulty:** Foundational

According to AFH 1, what two key aspects of individual financial responsibility support “fiscal fitness”?

A. Establishing a personal budget and effectively managing debt.  
B. Maximizing all allotments and declining SGLI coverage.  
C. Deferring LES review until PCS and avoiding myPay.  
D. Using only cash travel advances and skipping vouchers.  

**Correct answer: A**

**Rationale:** AFH 1 states fiscal fitness is as important as physical fitness and names establishing a personal budget and effective debt management as two key aspects.

**Why the other options are incorrect:**

- **B:** Allotment maximization and declining SGLI are not the two key aspects named.
- **C:** AFH 1 emphasizes regular LES review, not deferral.
- **D:** Timely vouchers and proper GTCC use are highlighted, not cash-only travel.

**Source:** AFH 1, ¶12.1  
**Review flag:** Standard citation verification
