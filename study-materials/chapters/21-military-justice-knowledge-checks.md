## Section 21A — Military Law

### C21-Q-001

- **ADTC:** 21A, B—Understanding
- **Ranks:** SSgt; TSgt
- **Difficulty:** Foundational

Why does AFH 1 say a special system of military laws and courts is required?

A. Because military discipline enhances combat capability, military duties are unique, and personnel serve throughout the world.
B. Because the Constitution forbids Congress from making any rules for the Armed Forces.
C. Because nonjudicial punishment is the only authorized form of discipline.
D. Because civilian courts refuse all cases involving service members worldwide.

**Correct answer: A**

**Rationale:** Paragraph 21.1 ties the special military justice system to combat capability, unique duties/environment, and worldwide service to maintain good order and discipline.

**Why the other options are incorrect:**

- **B:** Congress is empowered to make rules for the land and naval forces.
- **C:** NJP is one tool; courts-martial also exist.
- **D:** The source does not claim civilian courts refuse all service-member cases.

**Source:** AFH 1, ¶21.1
**Review flag:** Domain purpose; revalidate wording
