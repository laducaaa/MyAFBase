## Section 9A — Promotion Systems and Programs

### C09-Q-001

- **ADTC:** 9A, B—Understanding
- **Ranks:** SSgt; TSgt
- **Difficulty:** Foundational

Which goals of DoD Directive 1304.20 does AFH 1 say the enlisted promotion system supports?

A. Providing visible, relatively stable career progression opportunities; attracting, retaining, and motivating needed people; and ensuring uniform application of equal pay for equal work among the services.  
B. Restricting promotions to joint assignments only and eliminating equal-pay principles across services.  
C. Replacing all enlisted promotions with random selection and removing career-progression visibility.  
D. Limiting promotion systems exclusively to officer EPMS without enlisted application.  

**Correct answer: A**

**Rationale:** AFH 1 states the enlisted promotion system supports DoD Directive 1304.20 by providing visible, relatively stable career progression opportunities; attracting, retaining, and motivating the people the military needs; and ensuring uniform application of equal pay for equal work among the services.

**Why the other options are incorrect:**

- **B:** The source supports equal-pay application among the services, not its elimination.
- **C:** The system is designed for fair, recognizable career progression, not random selection.
- **D:** The paragraph addresses enlisted promotion systems supporting EPMS goals, not officer-only application.

**Source:** AFH 1, ¶9.1  
**Review flag:** Standard citation verification
