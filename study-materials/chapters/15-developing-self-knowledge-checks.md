### C15-Q-001

- **ADTC:** 15A, B—Understanding
- **Ranks:** SSgt; TSgt
- **Difficulty:** Foundational

How does AFH 1 define accountability?

A. Demonstrating reliability and honesty and taking responsibility for the behaviors of self and team.  
B. Completing PME courses on schedule regardless of personal conduct.  
C. Delegating all team mistakes to subordinates while protecting personal reputation.
