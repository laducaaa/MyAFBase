## Section 19A — Way of Life

### C19-Q-001

- **ADTC:** 19A, B—Understanding
- **Ranks:** SSgt; TSgt
- **Difficulty:** Foundational

According to AFH 1, when must USAF employees comply with prescribed standards of conduct?

A. Only during duty hours and only for military members.  
B. In all official matters, as well as when off-duty.  
C. Only while deployed under General Orders.  
D. Only after assuming a supervisory position.

**Correct answer: B**

**Rationale:** ¶19.1 states USAF employees must comply with prescribed standards of conduct in all official matters, as well as when off-duty.

**Why the other options are incorrect:**

- **A:** Standards apply off-duty and to military and civilian Airmen.
- **C:** General Orders are a deployed-discipline tool; standards are broader.
- **D:** Standards apply to all employees, not only supervisors.

**Source:** AFH 1, ¶19.1  
**Review flag:** Standard citation verification
