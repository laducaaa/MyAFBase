## Section 23A — Professional Image

### Core B—Understanding cards (SSgt and TSgt)


### C23-FC-001
- **Prompt:** Why does professional military image matter per AFH 1 (citing AFI 1-1)?
- **Answer:** First impressions are often based on appearance; professional image matters as much as professional behavior; pride enhances esprit de corps and reflects on the individual and USAF on and off duty.
- **Source:** AFH 1, ¶23.1
- **Tags:** SSgt; TSgt; 23A; B—Understanding

### C23-FC-002
- **Prompt:** What tobacco rule applies in uniform?
- **Answer:** Do not smoke or use smokeless tobacco products except in designated smoking areas when authorized.
- **Source:** AFH 1, ¶23.1
- **Tags:** SSgt; TSgt; 23A; B—Understanding

### C23-FC-003
- **Prompt:** May personal electronic devices be used while walking in uniform?
- **Answer:** Yes (phones, earpieces, speaker phones, text messaging), but military customs and courtesies take precedence.
- **Source:** AFH 1, ¶23.1
- **Tags:** SSgt; TSgt; 23A; B—Understanding

### C23-FC-004
- **Prompt:** What are the food and beverage rules while walking in uniform?
- **Answer:** No food while walking; beverages authorized; commanders may authorize food during special functions; customs/courtesies take precedence.
- **Source:** AFH 1, ¶23.1
- **Tags:** SSgt; TSgt; 23A; B—Understanding

### C23-FC-005
- **Prompt:** When is PDA briefly permitted?
- **Answer:** Commonly accepted etiquette situations such as weddings, graduations, promotions, retirements, or departure for/return from deployment.
- **Source:** AFH 1, ¶23.1
- **Tags:** SSgt; TSgt; 23A; B—Understanding

### C23-FC-006
- **Prompt:** What five elements comprise dress and personal appearance standards?
- **Answer:** Neatness, cleanliness, safety, uniformity, and military image.
- **Source:** AFH 1, ¶23.2
- **Tags:** SSgt; TSgt; 23A; B—Understanding

### C23-FC-007
- **Prompt:** Who must comply with and respectfully enforce dress standards?
- **Answer:** Every Airman, regardless of position or rank.
- **Source:** AFH 1, ¶23.2
- **Tags:** SSgt; TSgt; 23A; B—Understanding

### C23-FC-008
- **Prompt:** May more restrictive appearance standards be imposed?
- **Answer:** Yes, on or off duty for highly visible special mission requirements or special duty positions.
- **Source:** AFH 1, ¶23.2
- **Tags:** SSgt; TSgt; 23A; B—Understanding

### C23-FC-009
- **Prompt:** What overarching hair standards apply regardless of style?
- **Answer:** Conservative, clean, well-groomed, professional; within length/bulk limits; allow headgear/helmet/mask; no excessive grooming aids; not lopsided; not touch either eyebrow (exception: female bangs); not end below forehead line at top of eyebrows.
- **Source:** AFH 1, ¶23.3
- **Tags:** SSgt; TSgt; 23A; B—Understanding

### C23-FC-010
- **Prompt:** What hair colors result from authorized dyes/tints/bleaches/frostings?
- **Answer:** Natural hair colors with a natural appearance, complementary to facial hair (e.g., brown, blonde, brunette, natural red, gray, black).
- **Source:** AFH 1, ¶23.3
- **Tags:** SSgt; TSgt; 23A; B—Understanding

### C23-FC-011
- **Prompt:** Name unauthorized hair colors/styles highlighted in AFH 1.
- **Answer:** Colors: burgundy, purple, orange, fluorescent/neon (not all-inclusive), unnatural ombre/contrasts. Styles: Mohawk, mullet, etched design; males—no dreadlocks/coils/braids/twists/designs/extensions; females—no partially shaved sides/back with long top hair requiring ponytail/bun/braid.
- **Source:** AFH 1, ¶23.3
- **Tags:** SSgt; TSgt; 23A; B—Understanding

### C23-FC-012
- **Prompt:** What are male hair bulk and termination limits?
- **Answer:** Bulk not exceed 2 ½ inches regardless of length; 1/4-inch at natural termination; only closely cut/shaved neck hair may touch collar.
- **Source:** AFH 1, ¶23.3
- **Tags:** SSgt; TSgt; 23A; B—Understanding

### C23-FC-013
- **Prompt:** What limits apply to a male cut/clipped/shaved part?
- **Answer:** One front-to-back straight-line part; not slanted/curved; ≤4 inches length and ≤1/4-inch width.
- **Source:** AFH 1, ¶23.3
- **Tags:** SSgt; TSgt; 23A; B—Understanding

### C23-FC-014
- **Prompt:** What mustache limits apply?
- **Answer:** Not below upper lip line; not beyond horizontal line across mouth corners; ≤1/4-inch beyond vertical line from mouth corner; natural color match; no handlebars/twist/curls/goatee.
- **Source:** AFH 1, ¶23.3
- **Tags:** SSgt; TSgt; 23A; B—Understanding

### C23-FC-015
- **Prompt:** When are beards authorized and what is the medical length limit?
- **Answer:** Only for medical authorization or religious accommodation. Medical beards trimmed evenly, ≤1/4-inch; no goatee/fade shaping; not bleached/dyed.
- **Source:** AFH 1, ¶23.3
- **Tags:** SSgt; TSgt; 23A; B—Understanding

### C23-FC-016
- **Prompt:** What is maximum female hair bulk (and unshorn-hair religious accommodation)?
- **Answer:** 4 inches from the scalp; no minimum length; must allow proper headgear.
- **Source:** AFH 1, ¶23.3
- **Tags:** SSgt; TSgt; 23A; B—Understanding

### C23-FC-017
- **Prompt:** What ponytail/long-braid length limit applies (non-PTG)?
- **Answer:** Not below the horizontal line between top of each sleeve inseam at underarm through the shoulder blades; down the back only—not over the shoulder or in front.
- **Source:** AFH 1, ¶23.3
- **Tags:** SSgt; TSgt; 23A; B—Understanding

### C23-FC-018
- **Prompt:** What PTG exception applies to long hair?
- **Answer:** Hair will be secured but may have loose ends and may extend below the shoulder-blade horizontal line.
- **Source:** AFH 1, ¶23.3
- **Tags:** SSgt; TSgt; 23A; B—Understanding

### C23-FC-019
- **Prompt:** What female hair-accessory limits apply?
- **Answer:** Black or match hair color; headbands/scrunchies ≤2 inches wide; no ornaments (ribbons, beads, jeweled pins).
- **Source:** AFH 1, ¶23.3
- **Tags:** SSgt; TSgt; 23A; B—Understanding

### C23-FC-020
- **Prompt:** What dimension rules apply to locs/braids/twists/cornrows?
- **Answer:** Uniform dimension; braid/twist ≤1 inch wide with natural spacing; multiple locs/braids/twists ≤1/4-inch diameter with ≤1/4-inch scalp between; tightly interwoven; continue to end without design (micro-braids/twists excepted).
- **Source:** AFH 1, ¶23.3
- **Tags:** SSgt; TSgt; 23A; B—Understanding

### C23-FC-021
- **Prompt:** What fingernail length and polish rules apply?
- **Answer:** ≤1/4-inch beyond fingertip; clean/well groomed; no duty/safety interference. Males—no polish. Females—single conservative color; no designs/two-tone/multi-tone except white-tip French manicure.
- **Source:** AFH 1, ¶23.3
- **Tags:** SSgt; TSgt; 23A; B—Understanding

### C23-FC-022
- **Prompt:** What eyelash-extension limits apply?
- **Answer:** Natural color; ≤14 mm total length; not touch eyebrow; natural appearance; must not hinder protective eyewear/headgear.
- **Source:** AFH 1, ¶23.3
- **Tags:** SSgt; TSgt; 23A; B—Understanding

### C23-FC-023
- **Prompt:** Where are tattoos/brands/body markings unauthorized?
- **Answer:** Head, face, tongue, lips, eyes, and scalp.
- **Source:** AFH 1, ¶23.3
- **Tags:** SSgt; TSgt; 23A; B—Understanding

### C23-FC-024
- **Prompt:** What tattoo/brand content is unauthorized in and out of uniform?
- **Answer:** Obscene; gang/extremist/supremacist associations; sexual/racial/ethnic/religious discrimination; content prejudicial to good order and discipline or tending to bring discredit upon the DAF.
- **Source:** AFH 1, ¶23.3
- **Tags:** SSgt; TSgt; 23A; B—Understanding

### C23-FC-025
- **Prompt:** May unauthorized-content tattoos be covered with bandages or make-up to comply?
- **Answer:** No.
- **Source:** AFH 1, ¶23.3
- **Tags:** SSgt; TSgt; 23A; B—Understanding

### C23-FC-026
- **Prompt:** What neck and hand tattoo size/location limits apply?
- **Answer:** One neck tattoo ≤1 inch any direction, only behind ear-orifice vertical lines around the back (includes behind ear). Ring tattoos: one band per hand, ≤3/8-inch wide. Plus one hand tattoo per hand ≤1 inch any direction.
- **Source:** AFH 1, ¶23.3
- **Tags:** SSgt; TSgt; 23A; B—Understanding

### C23-FC-027
- **Prompt:** On what form are tattoo ETPs documented, and are content ETPs considered?
- **Answer:** DAF Form 4428. Unauthorized-content ETPs will not be considered.
- **Source:** AFH 1, ¶23.3
- **Tags:** SSgt; TSgt; 23A; B—Understanding

### C23-FC-028
- **Prompt:** What piercing prohibition applies in uniform and civilian attire on official duty?
- **Answer:** Except earrings per DAFI 36-2903 ¶7.3.1.1, no objects/jewelry/ornamentation through ear, nose, tongue, eyebrows, lips, or any exposed body part (including visible through the uniform).
- **Source:** AFH 1, ¶23.4
- **Tags:** SSgt; TSgt; 23A; B—Understanding

### C23-FC-029
- **Prompt:** What dental ornamentation rule and 13 June 2011 exemption apply?
- **Answer:** No designs/jewels/initials; ornamental gold/platinum caps not for medical/dental necessity prohibited. Documented permanent ornamental caps received before 13 June 2011 are exempted; no other dental ornamentation is exempted.
- **Source:** AFH 1, ¶23.4
- **Tags:** SSgt; TSgt; 23A; B—Understanding

### C23-FC-030
- **Prompt:** What body alterations/modifications are prohibited?
- **Answer:** Intentional alterations causing visible disfigurement/deformity or detracting from professional military image (e.g., tongue splitting/forking, tooth filing, visible disfiguring implants, gauging that permits light through).
- **Source:** AFH 1, ¶23.5
- **Tags:** SSgt; TSgt; 23A; B—Understanding

### C23-FC-031
- **Prompt:** What UCMJ article is cited for failing to obey mandatory tattoo/body-alteration provisions?
- **Answer:** Article 92(1), UCMJ (with Title 32 ANG notes as printed).
- **Source:** AFH 1, ¶¶23.3, 23.5
- **Tags:** SSgt; TSgt; 23A; B—Understanding

### C23-FC-032
- **Prompt:** Are wigs/hairpieces/extensions authorized? Any male restriction?
- **Answer:** Authorized if they meet natural-hair standards and safety/professional requirements. Extensions prohibited for males; not used to cover unauthorized styles.
- **Source:** AFH 1, ¶23.3
- **Tags:** SSgt; TSgt; 23A; B—Understanding

### C23-FC-033
- **Prompt:** What sideburn limits apply?
- **Answer:** Straight and even width (not flared); not below bottom of ear orifice; end in clean-shaven horizontal line.
- **Source:** AFH 1, ¶23.3
- **Tags:** SSgt; TSgt; 23A; B—Understanding

### C23-FC-034
- **Prompt:** What cosmetic rules apply to males and females?
- **Answer:** Males—not authorized. Females—conservative cosmetics; conservative lipstick; no winged eyeliner past eye corners; no cosmetics in field conditions.
- **Source:** AFH 1, ¶23.3
- **Tags:** SSgt; TSgt; 23A; B—Understanding

### C23-FC-035
- **Prompt:** What chest/back tattoo visibility rule applies?
- **Answer:** Not visible through any uniform combination or while wearing an open-collar uniform.
- **Source:** AFH 1, ¶23.3
- **Tags:** SSgt; TSgt; 23A; B—Understanding

### C23-FC-036
- **Prompt:** What applicant hand-tattoo ETP authority exists?
- **Answer:** Component senior waiver authority may allow hand tattoos ≤25% of the hand for exceptionally qualified applicants in undermanned critical fields; content restrictions still apply; document on DAF Form 4428.
- **Source:** AFH 1, ¶23.3
- **Tags:** SSgt; TSgt; 23A; B—Understanding

### C23-FC-037
- **Prompt:** May commanders be more restrictive on tattoos/ornaments/grooming?
- **Answer:** Yes, based on legal, safety, sanitary, and/or host-nation agreements (DAFI 36-2903 ¶2.14.4).
- **Source:** AFH 1, ¶23.3
- **Tags:** SSgt; TSgt; 23A; B—Understanding

### C23-FC-038
- **Prompt:** If DoD tattoo-removal resources are unavailable, who pays, and is permissive TDY authorized?
- **Answer:** Member expense outside DoD facilities; permissive TDY not authorized for this purpose.
- **Source:** AFH 1, ¶23.3
- **Tags:** SSgt; TSgt; 23A; B—Understanding

### C23-FC-039
- **Prompt:** What pulled-back hair width/bulk limits apply?
- **Answer:** ≤6 inches radius left or right (12 inches total width) and 6 inches bulk; allow headgear; not gathered at crown or worn on top of head.
- **Source:** AFH 1, ¶23.3
- **Tags:** SSgt; TSgt; 23A; B—Understanding

### C23-FC-040
- **Prompt:** How does AFH 1 define a tattoo for DAFI 36-2903 purposes?
- **Answer:** A picture, design, or marking made by indelible dye or any other method, including markings only visible under certain conditions (e.g., UV/invisible ink).
- **Source:** AFH 1, ¶23.3
- **Tags:** SSgt; TSgt; 23A; B—Understanding


## Section 23B — Military Uniforms

### Core B—Understanding cards (SSgt and TSgt)


### C23-FC-041
- **Prompt:** What five standards must members in uniform meet?
- **Answer:** Neatness, cleanliness, safety, uniformity, and military image; members procure/maintain mandatory items and display correct placement of ribbons/insignia.
- **Source:** AFH 1, ¶23.6
- **Tags:** SSgt; TSgt; 23B; B—Understanding

### C23-FC-042
- **Prompt:** Who wears formal vs mess dress?
- **Answer:** Formal: officers only. Mess: mandatory officers, optional enlisted. No nametag; saluting not required.
- **Source:** AFH 1, ¶23.7
- **Tags:** SSgt; TSgt; 23B; B—Understanding

### C23-FC-043
- **Prompt:** Enlisted alternate if no mess dress?
- **Answer:** Semi-formal service dress.
- **Source:** AFH 1, ¶23.7
- **Tags:** SSgt; TSgt; 23B; B—Understanding

### C23-FC-044
- **Prompt:** Medals on formal/mess dress?
- **Answer:** All miniature medals on wearer’s left between lapel and arm seam, midway between top shoulder seam and top button.
- **Source:** AFH 1, ¶23.7
- **Tags:** SSgt; TSgt; 23B; B—Understanding

### C23-FC-045
- **Prompt:** Semi-formal coat and saluting?
- **Answer:** Service dress coat without nametag; saluting not required.
- **Source:** AFH 1, ¶23.8
- **Tags:** SSgt; TSgt; 23B; B—Understanding

### C23-FC-046
- **Prompt:** Class A vs Class B?

- **Tags:** SSgt; TSgt; 23B; B—Understanding

### C23-FC-047
- **Prompt:** U.S. lapel insignia officer vs enlisted?

- **Tags:** SSgt; TSgt; 23B; B—Understanding

### C23-FC-048

- **Source:** AFH 1, ¶23.9
- **Tags:** SSgt; TSgt; 23B; B—Understanding

### C23-FC-049
- **Prompt:** Tie/tie tab long vs short sleeve?

- **Source:** AFH 1, ¶23.9
- **Tags:** SSgt; TSgt; 23B; B—Understanding
