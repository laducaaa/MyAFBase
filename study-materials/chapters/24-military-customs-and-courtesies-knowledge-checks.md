### C24-Q-001

- **ADTC:** 24A, B—Understanding
- **Ranks:** SSgt; TSgt
- **Difficulty:** Foundational

According to AFH 1, what role do military customs and courtesies play?

A. They build morale, esprit de corps, discipline, and mission effectiveness and reflect the unique nature of the profession.  
B. They replace all written regulations for every duty section.  
C. They apply only during wartime overseas deployments.  
D. They prohibit mutual respect for civilian leadership.  

**Correct answer: A**

**Rationale:** AFH 1 states customs and courtesies play an extremely important role in building morale, esprit de corps, discipline, and mission effectiveness and reflect the profession’s unique nature.

**Why the other options are incorrect:**

- **B:** Customs guide behavior; they do not erase written directives.
- **C:** They apply broadly, not only in wartime overseas.
- **D:** They emphasize mutual respect for one another and civilian leadership.

**Source:** AFH 1, ¶24.1

A. The statue is a testimony to military courtesies, dress and appearance, and drill and ceremonies—and most importantly represents that people are valued most.  
B. Courtesies exist only to decorate ceilings with the flag.  
C. Courtesies require dipping the U.S. flag to every visitor.

**Correct answer: A**

**Rationale:** AFH 1 ties the Honor Guard Statue to courtesies/drill and valuing people.

**Why the other options are incorrect:**

- **B:** Flag-as-ceiling is prohibited.
- **C:** U.S. flag not dipped.
- **D:** No-salute areas do not require saluting.
