## Section 18A — Integrated Defense

### C18-Q-001

- **ADTC:** 18A, B—Understanding
- **Ranks:** SSgt; TSgt
- **Difficulty:** Foundational

According to AFH 1, what is the purpose of security?

A. To never permit the enemy to acquire an unexpected advantage.  
B. To replace emergency management and antiterrorism programs.  
C. To apply only to Security Forces while deployed.  
D. To authorize classification of all installation information as Secret.  

**Correct answer: A**

**Rationale:** AFH 1 states the purpose of security is to never permit the enemy to acquire an unexpected advantage.

**Why the other options are incorrect:**

- **B:** Integrated defense coordinates with other programs; it does not replace them.
- **C:** Security applies to all USAF members at all times.
- **D:** Classification levels are addressed in Section 18C; this is not the purpose of security.

**Source:** AFH 1, ¶18.1  
**Review flag:** Standard citation verification
