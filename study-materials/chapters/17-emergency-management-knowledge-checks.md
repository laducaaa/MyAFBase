## Section 17A — Air Force Emergency Management

### C17-Q-001

- **ADTC:** 17A, B—Understanding
- **Ranks:** SSgt; TSgt
- **Difficulty:** Foundational

According to AFH 1, the DAF EM Program was developed to ensure readiness of forces to accomplish which set of outcomes?

A. Replace antiterrorism programs and host-nation support with installation fire response only.  
B. Suspend all operations until every hazard is eliminated worldwide.  
C. Limit EM exclusively to post-incident paperwork after Class A mishaps.  
D. Save lives; minimize loss or degradation of resources; and continue, sustain, and restore operational capability.  

**Correct answer: D**

**Rationale:** AFH 1 states the EM Program ensures readiness to save lives; minimize loss or degradation of resources; and continue, sustain, and restore operational capability in all-hazards environments.

**Why the other options are incorrect:**

- **A:** Ancillary mission includes AT and civil/host-nation support; EM is not fire-only.
- **B:** EM enables mission continuance under adversity, not worldwide suspension.
- **C:** EM spans prevent through recover; it is not limited to mishap paperwork.


**Source:** AFH 1, ¶17.1  
**Review flag:** Standard citation verification
