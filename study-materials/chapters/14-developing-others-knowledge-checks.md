### C14-Q-001

- **ADTC:** 14A, B—Understanding
- **Ranks:** SSgt; TSgt
- **Difficulty:** Foundational

According to AFH 1, what is at the core of all healthy team interaction?

A. Trust.  
B. Formal authority alone.  
C. Unhelpful competition among members.  
D. Avoiding all disagreement.  

**Correct answer: A**

**Rationale:** AFH 1 states healthy teams most often have trust, communication, and cooperation as a foundation, and trust is at the core of all healthy team interaction.

**Why the other options are incorrect:**

- **B:** B: Formal authority is not identified as the core of healthy team interaction.
- **C:** C: Unhelpful competition is a sign the team is not performing cohesively.
- **D:** D: Conflict can be constructive; avoiding all disagreement is not the trust foundation.

**Source:** AFH 1, ¶14.1
