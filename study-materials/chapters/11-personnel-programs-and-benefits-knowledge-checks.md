### C11-Q-001

- **ADTC:** 11A, B—Understanding
- **Ranks:** SSgt; TSgt
- **Difficulty:** Foundational

Why does AFH 1 say Airmen need to understand benefits and services?

A. Only finance technicians need benefits knowledge because Airmen receive automatic entitlements without personal responsibility.  
B. Benefits apply only after retirement, so active-duty Airmen need not study them.  
C. Airmen need to understand benefits applicable to themselves and to subordinates, peers, and leaders, taking responsibility and serving as good wingmen while remaining mission-ready.  
D. Benefits knowledge is required only for M&FRC staff, not for unit Airmen.  

**Correct answer: C**

**Rationale:** AFH 1 opens by linking mission readiness to understanding benefits/services for self and others and serving as good wingmen.

**Why the other options are incorrect:**

- **A:** Responsibility is not limited to finance technicians.
- **B:** Benefits and services apply throughout careers, not only after retirement.
- **D:** AFH 1 addresses Airmen generally, not only M&FRC staff.

**Source:** AFH 1, ¶11.1
