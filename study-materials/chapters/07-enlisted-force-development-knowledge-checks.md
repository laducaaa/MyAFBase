## Section 7A — Leadership Levels

### C07-Q-001

- **ADTC:** 7A, B—Understanding
- **Ranks:** SSgt; TSgt
- **Difficulty:** Foundational

Why does AFH 1 say officers, civilians, and enlisted Airmen need a deliberate and common approach to force development?

A. Many enlisted Airmen have officers or civilians as direct supervisors, so all three groups need a shared approach to force development, career progression, and increased supervisory and leadership responsibilities.  
B. Only enlisted Airmen require deliberate force development because officers and civilians follow separate systems without overlapping supervision.  
C. Civilians alone set force-development policy, so officers and enlisted Airmen need only occupational training.  
D. A common approach is required only after Airmen reach the strategic vision leadership level.  

**Correct answer: A**

**Rationale:** AFH 1 opens Section 7A by noting that many enlisted Airmen have officers or civilians as direct supervisors and that all three groups need a deliberate, common approach to force development, career progression, and assuming increased supervisory and leadership responsibilities.

**Why the other options are incorrect:**

- **B:** The source expressly includes officers and civilians in the common-approach requirement, not enlisted Airmen alone.
- **C:** Civilians are part of the shared need; they do not uniquely set force-development policy in this paragraph.
- **D:** The common approach is not deferred until the strategic vision level.

**Source:** AFH 1, ¶7.1
